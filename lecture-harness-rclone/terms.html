Terms of use

lecture-harness-rclone is a personal tool for connecting the owner's Google Drive to an authorized course-material workflow. It is provided for the operator's personal use and is not offered as a public hosted service.

Authorized use

Use the integration only with accounts and materials you are authorized to access and process. Google Drive operations are performed according to the owner's selected account, configured directories, and instructions. The owner remains responsible for backups, provider terms, and permissions for course materials.

Availability and output

The integration depends on Google and the owner's infrastructure; continuous availability is not guaranteed. Generated lecture notes should be checked against original materials. Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections.
