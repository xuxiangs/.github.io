Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal lecture-harness-rclone Google Drive integration operated by Xuxiang.

Data accessed and purposes

With the owner's consent, rclone accesses Google Drive file and folder names, identifiers, metadata, and contents, and the account identity needed to confirm the correct Drive account. Full Drive permission enables access to existing course files and publication of completed notes. Operations are limited to the owner's configured workflow and explicit requests.

Storage and security

OAuth client credentials and access and refresh tokens are stored in restricted files on the owner's computer or server. They are not published on this website or committed to source control. Transfers use encrypted connections. Downloaded materials and generated outputs are stored on the owner's configured computer, server, and Google Drive.

Processing and disclosure

The rclone OAuth integration transports files. The separate lecture-harness application may send selected course material to OCR or language-model services configured and authorized by the owner to produce requested notes. Those services have their own applicable terms and data policies. This integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or intentionally share course files with other Drive accounts. Google user data is not used by this integration to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

lecture-harness-rclone's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

The owner controls retention of local materials, generated outputs, and credentials. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase files already stored on the owner's computer, server, or Drive. The owner may delete those copies and credentials directly. Contact the operator for questions or deletion assistance.

This website

This static site contains application information only. It does not collect OAuth credentials or course materials and includes no app analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy policy.
